01 — Foundations

A warm room, after dark

BG Hub lives in dark mode by default. The surfaces are warm — felt-table greens, walnut-wood browns, candle-amber accents — never cold or clinical. Every surface should feel liftable, like a tile or card you could pick up.

Construction notes

  • Mark: a flat hex (the universal boardgame tile) holding a single meeple silhouette — collection + community in one shape.
  • Wordmark: Bricolage Grotesque 700, "Hub" italic for warmth, tracked tight (-2%).
  • Clear space: the height of the meeple's head all around the lockup.
  • Min size: mark 20px / lockup 100px wide.
